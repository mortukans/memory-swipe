Memory Swipe: Photo Cleanup

Room for
more.

Revisit your photos one at a time. Keep what matters. Make room for more. Free, private, and entirely on your iPhone.

How it works

  • Swipe right to keep a photo or video. Swipe left to set it aside.
  • Nothing is deleted by a swipe. Everything you set aside waits in Review for one last look.
  • Deletion only happens after you confirm, and iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.
  • Short sessions of up to 20 memories. Undo and Skip are always one tap away.
  • No account, no uploads, no ads. Your library never leaves your device.
