2. Purpose and target audience
Memory Swipe helps iPhone users who have thousands of photos and a full library tidy it up in small, calm sessions while rediscovering old memories. Photos are shown one at a time: swipe right to keep, left to set aside. Nothing is deleted by a swipe; everything set aside waits on a Review screen and is only deleted after the user confirms in the app and then in the standard iOS deletion dialog (items go to Recently Deleted for 30 days). An optional "On this day" view and daily reminder show photos taken on the same date in past years. Target audience: everyday iPhone users of any age who want more free space and a gentle way to look back at their photos.

3. Setup and main features
No account, login or sample files are needed.
- Launch the app and tap "Choose photo access", then allow access (full or selected photos).
- Discover: tap "Surprise me" for a session of up to 20 photos, or choose Photos, Videos, or "On this day".
- Swipe right or tap Keep to keep; swipe left or tap Remove to set aside. Undo and Skip are always available. The heart on a card adds the photo to Favourites in the Photos app.
- Review tab: tap a photo to restore it, or "Delete N items" to delete; iOS shows its own confirmation.
- Library tab: browse by month or album. Settings: language (English / Latvian), appearance, optional daily reminder.

4. External services
None. The app makes no network requests and uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, advertising, authentication, payments or AI services. It uses only Apple's Photos framework on the device and a local database for the user's keep/remove decisions. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

5. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in all regions. It is available in English and Latvian and follows the device language.

6. Regulated industry or third-party material
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no third-party content; it only displays the user's own photos.
